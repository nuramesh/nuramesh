# S2: Meeting load and output, internal engineering study (sample data, fictional)

Sample document bundled with the template. All figures are invented for the walkthrough.

Study of 18 engineering teams over 26 weeks.
Key finding: teams that cut recurring meetings by a third shipped 14% more completed tasks per sprint.
Key finding: onboarding time for new hires rose by 9 days on teams with no in-person overlap week.
Method note: teams chose their own meeting changes, so the groups are not randomized.
