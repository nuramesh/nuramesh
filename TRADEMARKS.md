# NuraMesh trademark policy

NuraMesh is open-source software, and we want people to talk about it, build on it and share it. We also want the name and logo to mean one thing: software that really comes from the NuraMesh project. This page explains how you can use them.

## What this covers

- The name **NuraMesh** (the word mark).
- The **NuraMesh logo**.

NuraMesh is a trademark of AI Collective Labs Inc. This policy does not claim that the mark is registered anywhere.

The documentation text is licensed under CC BY 4.0 (code samples under Apache-2.0); that licence also does not cover the name or logo. This policy fills that gap.

The commercial parts of NuraMesh (products such as **NuraMesh Enterprise**) are proprietary to AI Collective Labs Inc. and are not part of the open-source release.

## Uses you may make without asking

You may use the NuraMesh name, as long as what you say is true and does not suggest that we endorse you:

- To refer to the software: "We use NuraMesh to verify our agents' work."
- To describe your own product or project: "built on NuraMesh", "compatible with NuraMesh", "a plugin for NuraMesh", "works with NuraMesh".
- In articles, blog posts, books, tutorials, videos, talks and course material about NuraMesh.
- When you redistribute **unmodified** NuraMesh releases (for example in a Linux distribution or a container image), under the same name, with the LICENSE and NOTICE files kept.
- For community meetups, user groups and online communities about NuraMesh, if you make clear that the group is independent, for example: "This is a community event and is not organised or endorsed by AI Collective Labs Inc."
- You may show the unmodified logo next to a truthful reference to NuraMesh, such as a link to the project or a "built on NuraMesh" badge.

When you do, use the name as it is spelled ("NuraMesh"), do not make it more prominent than your own name, and do not change the logo.

## Uses that need our written permission

Ask us first (see "Asking permission" below) before you:

- Use NuraMesh, or any part of it, in the name of a company, product, service, domain, package, repository, app or social media account (for example "NuraMesh Cloud" or "nuramesh-pro.io"). "nuramesh-plugin-xyz" style package names for add-ons are a common case; ask and we will usually say yes.
- Distribute a **modified** version under the NuraMesh name or logo.
- Make or sell merchandise (shirts, stickers, mugs) with the name or logo.
- Use the logo in any way other than an unmodified reference to the project, including in your own logo or in a changed form.
- Use names such as "NuraMesh Enterprise", "NuraMesh Cloud" or similar product names; these belong to our commercial products.
- Say that a product, service or person is "certified", "official", "approved" or "a partner" of NuraMesh.
- Say or imply that AI Collective Labs Inc. sponsors, endorses or is affiliated with you or your work.
- Run a paid event with NuraMesh in its name.

## Uses that are never allowed

- Any use that is false or misleading about who made the software or who you are.
- Names, logos or domains that are confusingly similar to NuraMesh (for example small spelling changes).
- Registering domains, package names or accounts with NuraMesh in them to hold them, resell them or block us.
- Presenting a fork or modified version as the official NuraMesh.
- Using the name or logo in a way that disparages NuraMesh or AI Collective Labs Inc., or in connection with illegal activity.
- Removing or changing the copyright, LICENSE or NOTICE information in a way that misrepresents where the software came from.

## Forks and modified versions

The Apache licence lets you fork and change the code. If you do, and you distribute the result:

1. Give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to NuraMesh.
2. Remove the NuraMesh logo from the product, its website, its packages and its user interface.
3. Change package names, container image names and command names that would make it look like official NuraMesh.
4. Keep the LICENSE and NOTICE files, as the Apache licence requires.
5. You may say truthfully where it came from: "based on NuraMesh" or "a fork of NuraMesh".

Changes that only adjust packaging for a distribution (paths, build flags, security patches taken from the project) do not count as a modified version for this purpose, as long as the software's behaviour is unchanged.

## Attribution and no endorsement

Where it fits, please add this line:

> NuraMesh is a trademark of AI Collective Labs Inc. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by AI Collective Labs Inc.

The second sentence is required for community groups, events and any project that names NuraMesh prominently.
